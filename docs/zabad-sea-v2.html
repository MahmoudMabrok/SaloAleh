The sea is set the moment you open it. Then it holds — until the wave.

Level, foam and murk are all sampled once from how long you've been silent, and then frozen — the sea does not creep upward while you sit there. Taps don't move the water either; they fill the counter. At 100 the wave takes over and washes the whole sea clean. Tap the phone to try it.

The sea only ever changes on two events: screen entry and a wash. Drag the hours slider, then hit re-open — that's what onScreenEntered() does. Sitting still changes nothing; tapping changes nothing but the number.

How the wave takes over at 100

Sweep: a whitecap rolls in from the side, scours the foam off the surface and dissolves it into rising light as the water drains back down. This is what ships today.
