What's inside

Everything you need to worship with intention

Six features built for daily engagement and weekly competition — simple to start, rewarding to stick with.

Weekly Competition

Every Friday at midnight Cairo time, the round resets and a new competition begins. Tap as many salawat as you can before the week ends.

Resets every Friday

Friday 2× Bonus

On the blessed day of Jumu'ah, every tap counts double. Your score multiplies on Fridays (Africa/Cairo timezone) to reward the holiest day.

2× multiplier

Global Leaderboard

Compete anonymously with believers worldwide. Your tag shows only your country and last 6 characters of a hashed ID — privacy first, glory second.

9 countries active

Rank Achievements

Finish in the top 10 of any completed round and earn a permanent rank badge. Place 1st through 10th — each rank has its own distinct badge.

10 rank badges

Streak Badges

Open the app every day and build your streak. Hit 7 days and 30 days to unlock special streak badges added to your achievements history.

7 & 30 day streaks

Daily Hadiths

Between rounds, browse a curated collection of authentic hadiths. A reminder of why we send salawat and the blessings it brings.
